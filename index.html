One half-orbit is visible above the horizon: sunrise to sunset takes half a revolution.

What this means: at 1 AU, the circle is about 940 million km around. At light speed, the Sun would sweep from horizon to horizon in about 26 minutes. The sky view shows light emitted about 8 minutes 19 seconds earlier. At constant speed, this delay shifts the apparent position without changing the crossing time. At c, the visible Sun is about 57.3° behind its current position.

This model assigns a speed to the Sun and ignores gravity, relativity, and Earth’s rotation. A real Sun cannot travel at light speed.
