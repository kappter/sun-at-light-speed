This is a geometric thought experiment: Earth is stationary, the orbit is circular, and gravity, relativity, and Earth's rotation are excluded. A real Sun or Moon cannot travel at light speed. Distances are measured from Earth's center; surface parallax, lunar phases, and illumination changes are not modeled. Planet layers show possible Earth-distance ranges using mean Sun distances in a circular, coplanar estimate. The Moon is treated as a visible marker.
